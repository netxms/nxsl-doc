[.nxsl-class]
[[class-coolingzone]]
=== CoolingZone

[.version-added]
Added in version 7.0

Object represents cooling zone - cooling plant or cooled area within a <<class-facility>>. Can be nested under another cooling zone. A rack may belong to at most one cooling zone. Extends <<class-datacollectiontarget>>.
